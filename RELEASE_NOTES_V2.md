# 🚀 Depup Office v2.2.0 Release Notes

**Depup Office v2.2.0** brings direct WiFi/Bluetooth printing, an offline Text-to-Speech Voice Reader, full Word/Excel editors, QR manager, and Google Play Store privacy compliance. Built for job seekers, office professionals, students, and businesses.

---

## 🌟 What's New in v2.2.0

### 🖨️ 1. Direct WiFi & Bluetooth Printing (`PrintHelper`)
* **1-Click Printing**: Print PDF documents, Word `.docx` files, Excel `.xlsx` spreadsheets, and photo scans directly to any WiFi/Bluetooth printer or Save as PDF via Android `PrintManager`.

### 🔊 2. Offline Document Voice Reader (`VoiceReaderManager`)
* **100% Offline Text-to-Speech**: Listen to PDFs, Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, or OCR-scanned text read out loud without internet.
* **Interactive Control Dialog**:
  * ⏯️ Play, Pause, Resume, Stop controls.
  * ⚡ Speech Speed Selector (`0.75x`, `1.0x`, `1.25x`, `1.5x`, `2.0x`).
  * 🇮🇩 / 🇬🇧 Language Switcher (Indonesian `id_ID` & English `en_US`).
  * Real-time paragraph reading progress and preview.

### 📝 3. Native Word & Excel Editors
* Full WYSIWYG Word `.docx` editor and Excel `.xlsx` spreadsheet editor with formula calculations (`SUM`, `AVERAGE`, `MIN`, `MAX`).

### 📷 4. Smart Scanner & Privacy Tools
* KTP 2-sided A4 layout, QR Code/Barcode Scanner & Generator, Eye-Care Reader Themes, Transparent Signature Extractor, and AES-256 Biometric Vault.

### 🛡️ 5. Play Store Compliance & Saweria Donation
* Includes local Privacy Policy Activity (`file:///android_asset/privacy.html`).
* Direct external Saweria donation integration (`https://saweria.co/depupdonate`).
