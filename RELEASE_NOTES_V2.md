# 🚀 Depup Office v2.1.0 Update Release Notes

**Depup Office v2.1.0** is an update delivering document editing, native Excel/Spreadsheet & Word file support, advanced mobile office productivity, smart AI-driven document scanning, and enhanced privacy tools. Built for job seekers, office professionals, students, and businesses in Indonesia and worldwide.

---

## 🌟 What's New in v2.1.0

### 📱 1. QR Code & Barcode Manager
* **QR Code & Barcode Scanner**: Instant scanning of QR codes and barcodes from photos or documents (e.g. e-Meterai verification, document links, barcodes).
* **Custom QR Code Generator**: Generate custom QR codes for websites, text, phone numbers, or document verification IDs. Save as PNG or stamp directly onto PDF pages.

### 🖋️ 2. Transparent PNG Signature & Stamp Extractor
* **Background Removal**: Turns photos of wet signatures or paper company stamps into transparent PNGs with 1 tap.
* **Ready-To-Stamp**: Extracted transparent signatures are immediately saved to Gallery and ready to stamp onto any PDF or Word document.

### 👁️ 3. Eye-Care Paper Reading Themes
* **4 Eye-Friendly Reading Themes**:
  * ☀️ **Day Mode** (Classic White)
  * 🌙 **Night Mode** (Inverted Dark `#0F172A`)
  * 📜 **Sepia Parchment** (Warm Book Paper `#FBF0D9`)
  * 🌿 **Eye-Care Soft Green** (Anti-Glare Soft Green `#E2F0D9` to reduce eye strain during long reading sessions)

### 📌 4. Smart Document Summary
* **Crucial Info Extraction**: Automatically parses OCR text to extract **Contract/Document Numbers**, **Crucial Dates**, and **Monetary Figures (Rp)** into a clear summary card with 1-click copy/share.

### 📜 5. Ready-To-Use Office Templates
* **Job Application Letter (Surat Lamaran Kerja)**: Pre-formatted HRD-standard letter template in Word (`.docx`).
* **CV / Resume**: Clean Curriculum Vitae structure ready to customize.
* **Receipt & Sales Invoice**: Pre-filled official payment receipt and invoice spreadsheet in Excel (`.xlsx`).

### 🪪 6. KTP / ID Card 2-Sided A4 Scanner
* **Guided 2-Side Capture**: Guides user to take Front & Back ID photos; automatically formats both onto 1 clean A4 page with official borders and header stamp.

### ⬛ 7. Blackout Redaction & Watermarking
* **Blackout / Redaction Tool**: Permanent solid black redaction boxes over sensitive NIK, phone number, and address fields.
* **Custom Watermark Stamping**: Diagonal semi-transparent custom watermarks (*"HANYA UNTUK VERIFIKASI"*, *"DOKUMEN RAHASIA"*, *"COPY"*).

### 💻 8. Fullscreen Presenter Mode + Laser Pointer
* **Slideshow Presentation**: Present PDF slides fullscreen in landscape mode.
* **Glowing Red Laser Pointer**: Interactive digital laser dot that follows finger movement across slides.

### ⚡ 9. Batch Converter & Gallery Photo Compressor
* **Batch Photo to PDF**: Convert dozens of photos into separate PDFs in a single batch operation.
* **Gallery Photo Compressor**: Reduce image sizes before sending via WhatsApp/Email with exact saved storage MB reports.

### 🔐 10. AES-256 Vault with Biometric Lock
* **Fingerprint / Biometric Security**: Open encrypted Vault Brankas using Fingerprint, Face, or Device Screen Lock.
